Why WAV?

  • DISTRIBUTORS REQUIRE ITMost distributors only accept WAV (16 or 24-bit, 44.1 kHz). MP3 uploads get rejected or re-encoded badly.
  • WHAT YOU GETStandard 16-bit / 44.1 kHz stereo WAV, the exact format stores expect.

Does DistroKid accept MP3?

DistroKid accepts MP3 but recommends WAV. TuneCore, CD Baby and most other distributors want WAV or FLAC. Uploading WAV avoids a second round of compression in the stores.

16-bit or 24-bit?

16-bit / 44.1 kHz is CD quality and is accepted everywhere. If your session exported 24-bit, keep that original: it's the better master.

Is my song uploaded anywhere?

No. The conversion happens in your browser using your own device, so unreleased music stays on your computer or phone.
