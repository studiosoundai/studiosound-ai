Billing questions.

What happens when I hit a limit?

That tool pauses until your limits reset on the 1st of the month, or you can upgrade and keep going right away. Everything else keeps working.

Do unused generations roll over?

No. Limits reset on the 1st of each month whether you used them or not.

Can I use anything without an account?

Yes. The cover resizer, MP3 to WAV converter, audio trimmer, split sheet, metadata formatter, release checklist and the forecast preview all work without signing up.

How does yearly billing work?

You pay once a year: $83.88 for Pro ($6.99 a month) or $203.88 for Premium ($16.99 a month). That's about 30% less than paying monthly.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from Settings and you keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

Can I switch plans?

Yes. Upgrade, downgrade or switch between monthly and yearly from the billing portal in Settings.

Is the forecast a guarantee?

No. It's three scenarios built from your own history. It shows where you're headed and what changes it, not a promise of income, and it isn't financial advice.

Who owns what I make?

You do. Covers, plans, lyrics and videos are yours to release, print and promote.
