Questions artists ask first.

Does it work with my distributor?

If your distributor lets you download a statement with dates and amounts, yes. We've tested DistroKid, TuneCore and Stop One reports, including Excel exports with over 140,000 rows. Upload the original file rather than a converted CSV, because conversions often round the cents away. You can also type your monthly totals in by hand.

Is my royalty report private?

Your file is read in your browser and never uploaded. When you run a full forecast, only your monthly totals, genre and city are sent to write your analyst report.

Is the forecast a guarantee?

No. It's three scenarios built from your own history and from how momentum usually fades after a release. It shows where you're headed and what changes it, not a promise of income, and it isn't financial advice.

My statements aren't in US dollars.

We detect the currency on your report. Forecast in USD at today's exchange rate, or keep your own currency with one tap.

Who owns what I make?

You do. Covers, plans, lyrics and videos are yours to release, print and promote.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from Settings and keep access until the end of your billing period.
