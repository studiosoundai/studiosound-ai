Quick answers.

How do I generate cover art?

Open the Studio, pick Cover Art, enter your title and describe your vision. You get two versions, one photoreal and one stylized, both 3000×3000 and ready for every store.

Who owns what I make?

You do. Covers, plans, lyrics, videos and ads are yours to release, print and promote.

How do I cancel or change my plan?

Go to Settings in the Studio and open the billing portal. You can cancel, switch plans or change between monthly and yearly, and you keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

When do my limits reset?

On the 1st of every month. Unused generations don't roll over.

Is there a free plan?

Yes, no card needed. It includes the forecast preview, 3 cover generations, 1 release plan a month, lyric videos, 2 merch mockups, 2 Meta ad kits and all the free tools. See pricing for every limit.

My lyric video won't save on my iPhone.

Export in Safari, then tap "Save to Photos / Share" and choose "Save Video". If you exported before October, export again: videos now save as MP4.

Which royalty report should I upload to the forecast?

The exact file your distributor gave you (CSV, TSV or Excel). Converted files often round the cents away. You can upload several and they stack.

How do I distribute through Stop One?

Apply on the distribution page. It's free to join and you keep 85% of your earnings.
