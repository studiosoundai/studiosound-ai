How far ahead should I upload my song?

Four weeks is the safe window. It gives your distributor time to deliver to every store and gives you time to pitch to Spotify's editorial team before release day.

What do I need before uploading?

A WAV master, a 3000×3000 cover, clean metadata with legal songwriter names, and a signed split sheet if anyone else worked on the song.

What should I do after release?

Keep going. Most independent songs pick up in weeks two to four as playlists and the algorithm test them, so keep posting and reacting to what listeners share.
